excerpt: 'I study whom local governments penalize when China''s central environmental inspectors return. Using case records for more than 80,000 citizen complaints across two inspection rounds, I find that shutdown orders for verified complaints against small service businesses fell from 45 to 22 percent, compared with 52 to 39 percent for heavy industry. The relative decline is about 36 percent and appears in all ten provinces, while the share of complaints found valid did not change.'
