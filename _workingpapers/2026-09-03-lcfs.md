excerpt: 'This paper asks what state clean-fuel standards add to federal biofuel mandates and how a newly adopting state should design one. It develops a numerical framework that jointly solves for fuel and feedstock market responses and for equilibrium in the federal Renewable Fuel Standard (RIN) and state LCFS credit markets, with lifecycle carbon accounting and blending limits. Counterfactuals evaluate California''s LCFS under the RFS and the expansion of LCFS coverage to additional states, and compare clean-fuel design features aimed at limiting leakage and increasing additionality.'
