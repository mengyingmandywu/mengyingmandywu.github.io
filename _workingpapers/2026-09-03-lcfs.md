excerpt: 'We study what state clean fuel standards add to federal biofuel mandates and how a state adopting one now should design it, in a model of fuel and vehicle markets with electric vehicle adoption and charging. In 2030, California''s standard cuts fuel emissions in the state by about 32 million tonnes, but about three quarters of that cut is offset in other states as existing biofuel moves into California. It raises California''s electric vehicle sales share from 21 to 33 percent. A new state that copies California''s standard sits at its price cap unless it adds other credit types or a looser target.'
