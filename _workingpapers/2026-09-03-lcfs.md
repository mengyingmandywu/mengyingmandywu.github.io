excerpt: 'This paper asks what California''s Low Carbon Fuel Standard achieves beyond the federal Renewable Fuel Standard, in emissions and in cost, and which design a newly adopting state should choose given that its choices interact with federal policy and with other states'' programs. It uses a multi-state dynamic model with endogenous electric-vehicle adoption and charging investment, and extends the incidence analysis within California, where LCFS revenue increasingly flows to utilities and charging providers while the cost is paid at the pump.'
