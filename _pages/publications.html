I study how climate and environmental policy performs once it passes through the markets and bureaucracies that deliver it: what it costs, who pays, and whether the change lasts. My work follows two lines, overlapping climate policies in U.S. transportation and delegated environmental enforcement in China, using equilibrium models, causal inference, and text-as-data methods.
